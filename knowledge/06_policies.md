# Clinic Policies at BrightSmile Dental Clinic

## Appointments and cancellation
- Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment.
- Cancel or reschedule at least 4 hours before your slot by calling or WhatsApp.
- If you are more than 20 minutes late, we may need to reschedule your appointment.
- No-show without notice more than twice: a 1,000 PKR booking advance will be required for future appointments.

## Advance payment
- Consultation and cleaning: no advance required.
- Braces, implants, crowns and veneers: a 40% advance is needed to confirm the treatment.

## Refunds
- Advance for a treatment not yet started is fully refundable within 7 days.
- Once a treatment has started, the amount for completed work is non-refundable.

## Emergency policy
- Emergencies (severe pain, swelling, knocked-out tooth, heavy bleeding) are seen the same day, even without an appointment, during clinic hours.
- Sunday and after-hours emergency: call 0300-1234567. The on-call dentist will advise you by phone.
- Emergencies have an extra fee of 1,000 PKR after 8:00 PM.

## Warranty
- Crowns and fillings: 1 year against defects from our side, if you attend follow-ups.
- Dental implants: 5 years on the implant, subject to regular check-ups.
- Braces: free adjustments during the treatment period.

## Hygiene and safety
- Autoclave sterilization of all instruments.
- Disposable gloves, masks, needles and suction tips for each patient.

## Privacy
- Patient records and X-rays are kept confidential and are shared only with the patient.

## Reports and records
- A digital copy of your X-ray and treatment record is sent to your WhatsApp on request.
