# Services Offered at BrightSmile Dental Clinic

## General dentistry
- Check-up and consultation: examination, digital X-ray if needed, and a treatment plan
- Scaling and polishing (teeth cleaning): removes plaque, tartar and surface stains. Takes about 30-40 minutes. Recommended every 6 months
- Fillings: tooth-colored composite fillings and silver amalgam fillings
- Tooth extraction: simple extraction and surgical extraction (including wisdom teeth)
- Root canal treatment (RCT): saves an infected tooth. Usually 1-2 visits. A crown is recommended afterwards

## Cosmetic dentistry
- Teeth whitening: in-clinic whitening session of about 60 minutes. Results last 1-2 years with good care
- Dental veneers: thin ceramic shells for chipped, stained or uneven teeth
- Smile design consultation

## Restorative treatments
- Dental crowns: zirconia and porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM)
- Bridges: replace one or more missing teeth
- Dentures: complete and partial
- Dental implants: titanium implant with crown. Needs a consultation and X-ray first

## Orthodontics
- Metal braces
- Ceramic (tooth-colored) braces
- Clear aligners (consultation and case assessment available)
- Retainers after braces

## Children's dentistry
- Child check-ups, fluoride application, fissure sealants
- Milk tooth fillings and extractions
- First dental visit is recommended around age 2-3

## Emergency care
- Severe toothache, swelling, broken tooth, knocked-out tooth and bleeding after extraction are treated as emergencies. See the policies document for emergency timings.
