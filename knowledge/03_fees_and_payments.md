# Fees and Payment at BrightSmile Dental Clinic

All prices are in Pakistani Rupees (PKR). Prices are starting rates and may vary depending on the case. The final fee is confirmed by the dentist after examination.

| Treatment | Fee (PKR) |
|---|---|
| Consultation / check-up | 1,500 (adjusted in treatment cost if you proceed the same day) |
| Digital X-ray (single) | 800 |
| Scaling and polishing | 4,000 |
| Composite (tooth-colored) filling | 4,000 - 6,000 per tooth |
| Silver amalgam filling | 2,500 per tooth |
| Simple tooth extraction | 3,000 |
| Surgical / wisdom tooth extraction | 8,000 - 15,000 |
| Root canal (front tooth) | 10,000 |
| Root canal (molar) | 15,000 - 18,000 |
| Zirconia crown | 18,000 per tooth |
| PFM crown | 10,000 per tooth |
| Teeth whitening (in-clinic) | 20,000 |
| Dental veneer (ceramic) | 25,000 per tooth |
| Metal braces (full treatment) | 90,000 - 120,000 |
| Ceramic braces (full treatment) | 150,000 - 180,000 |
| Dental implant (with crown) | 120,000 per tooth |
| Complete denture (per jaw) | 30,000 |
| Child check-up and fluoride | 2,000 |
| Child filling | 2,500 |

## Payment methods
- Cash
- Bank transfer (Meezan, HBL)
- JazzCash and Easypaisa
- Debit and credit cards accepted

## Installments
- Braces, implants and crowns can be paid in monthly installments. A 40% advance is required, and the rest is divided over 3-6 months.
- No interest is charged on installments.

## Discounts
- 10% discount for students (valid student ID required)
- 10% discount for senior citizens (60+)
- Family package: 15% off scaling when 3 or more family members come together
