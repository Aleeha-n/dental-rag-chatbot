# About BrightSmile Dental Clinic

BrightSmile Dental Clinic is a family dental clinic located in F-10 Markaz, Islamabad, Pakistan. The clinic has been serving patients since 2016 and treats both adults and children.

## What makes us different
- Modern sterilization: every instrument is sterilized in an autoclave after each patient
- Painless dentistry: we use digital anesthesia and numbing gel for nervous patients
- Digital X-rays: low radiation and results in under a minute
- Female dentist available on request
- Urdu and English speaking staff
