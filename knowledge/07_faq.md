# Frequently Asked Questions: BrightSmile Dental Clinic

## Is dental treatment painful?
Most treatments are done under local anesthesia, so you should not feel pain during the procedure. We use numbing gel before the injection for nervous patients. Mild soreness for a day or two after some procedures is normal.

## How often should I get my teeth cleaned?
Every 6 months is recommended. If you have gum problems, the dentist may advise every 3-4 months.

## Does scaling damage my teeth or make gaps?
No. Scaling removes tartar. Gaps may seem more visible after cleaning because swollen gums go down, but the teeth are not harmed.

## How long does teeth whitening last?
Typically 1-2 years. Tea, coffee, and smoking make it fade faster.

## How long does a root canal take?
One to two visits of about 45-60 minutes each. A crown is usually recommended afterwards to protect the tooth.

## How long do braces take?
Usually 12-24 months depending on the case. Visits are every 4-6 weeks for adjustment.

## Are dental implants safe? Who can get them?
Yes, implants are a well-established treatment. Most healthy adults with enough jawbone can get them. The oral surgeon decides after an X-ray. Smokers and uncontrolled diabetics have higher risk and need extra evaluation.

## What should I do after a tooth extraction?
Bite on the gauze for 30-45 minutes. Avoid hot food and drinks, rinsing hard, spitting and using a straw for 24 hours. Eat soft cold food. Take medicines as prescribed. If heavy bleeding continues for more than an hour, call the clinic immediately.

## What should I do if my tooth is knocked out?
Hold the tooth by the crown (not the root), do not scrub it, keep it in milk, and come to the clinic as fast as possible, ideally within 30 minutes.

We use digital X-rays that use very low radiation. They are safe when required for diagnosis. We inform pregnant patients to avoid unnecessary X-rays.
