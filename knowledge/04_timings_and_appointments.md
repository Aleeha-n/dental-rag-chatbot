## Public holidays
- The clinic is closed on Eid ul Fitr (3 days), Eid ul Adha (3 days), 14 August, and 9-10 Muharram.
- Holiday notice is shared on our WhatsApp status and Instagram a week in advance.

## Appointment slots
- Each appointment slot is 1 hour.
- Walk-ins are accepted but appointment holders are seen first, so walk-ins may have to wait.
- The quietest times are weekday mornings (9 AM to 12 PM).
- The busiest times are weekday evenings and all day Saturday.

## How to book
- Call or WhatsApp 0300-1234567, or use the chatbot on the website to request a slot.
- You need to share your name, phone number and preferred time.
